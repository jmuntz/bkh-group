# Brand Style Guide - BKH Group

## Personality
- Bold
- Modern
- Structural
- Confident
- Sparse copy
- Image-led

## Colour
- Ink: `#0a0a0a`
- Page: `#ececea`
- Surface: `#ffffff`
- Body text: `#5c5c58`
- Muted: `#8a8a86`
- Borders: `#d8d8d4`
- Main brand: `#5C4468`
- Accent / CTA: `#FFD300`
- Legacy accent (v1): `#6c5477`

Monochrome construction palette with brand purple and yellow. No purple gradients. No cream/terracotta editorial looks.

## Typography
- Display / H1: Syne ExtraBold (800)
- Subheadings: Outfit Light (300) at large scale
- Body: Outfit Light (300)

No serif fonts.

## Layout rules
- Full-bleed hero imagery
- Brand name is the hero-level signal
- One job per section
- Minimal text
- Prefer edge-to-edge media over cards
- Sharp corners on frames and buttons
